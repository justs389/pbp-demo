PALETTE
cream fdf3e0 · paper f2dcb6 · crust e8c58f · toast c98f4e · ink 2b1f14. Cream cheese is the pale fill, crust is the tan, poppy seeds are the ink dots.
MIX = THE SCHMEAR
The knob is a sliced bagel seen top-down. Mix spreads cream cheese clockwise from 6 o'clock all the way round to 6 o'clock again; bare crust keeps its poppy seeds. 0% is a dry bagel, 100% is fully covered. Cream cheese goes out to the rim at every setting, so only a sliver of seeded crust shows. The detune switch changes the topping: 3 plain, 6 scallion (green folded through the cheese), 9 piles on draped lox until it overtakes the bagel, a few scallion bits riding on top.
