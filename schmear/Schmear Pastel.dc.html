MIX = THE SCHMEAR
The knob is a sliced bagel seen top-down. Mix spreads cream cheese clockwise from 6 o'clock all the way round to 6 o'clock again; bare crust keeps its everything-bagel seeds. 0% is a dry bagel, 100% is fully covered. Cream cheese goes out to the rim at every setting, so only a sliver of seeded crust shows. The detune switch changes the topping: 3 plain, 6 scallion (green folded through the cheese), 9 piles on draped lox until it overtakes the bagel, a few scallion bits riding on top. The LOX / BERRY switch changes the flavor: LOX is the detune; BERRY is a vintage bucket-brigade chorus, the bagel turns cinnamon raisin and the cheese strawberry. I is strawberry cream cheese with red strawberry bits; II adds blueberries on top. BUMP is a little low-end lift under the chorus, drawn as a heaped schmear. HISS is the chorus's own noise: 50% is the real unit, 0 is none, and the small button beside it switches it off.
