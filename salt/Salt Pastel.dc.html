PIZZA BAGEL PLUGINS
V1.0.0
KOSHER — ACID / FAT
Acid: the box front — deep navy body (223163 → 131c36) under a yellow band f0c25c, salt-white line work. Fat swaps them: the same yellow f0c25c becomes the whole panel and the navy shrinks to the band, arcs and active cells. No new hues, just reversed weights.
PINK — ACID / FAT
Acid: daylight rock — warm ink 4a2119 on pale peach f6cdb8 / fdf1ea, with the dish and both knob faces in the band's own salmon e79a7c so the hardware matches the top banner. Fat swaps them: the peach band e79a7c becomes the whole panel and the pale paper fdf1ea becomes the band, arcs and active cells. Four themes — curve picks the salt, mode swaps band and body.
HEAT = THE PINCH DISH
Heat is the only big object on the panel: a salt dish seen top-down. The salt bed is fixed — a full pinch at every setting — and the value is carried by the arc, which reads from 12 o'clock, growing right for + and left for −. Kosher holds coarse flakes, Pink fine rose grains, so the curve choice is visible in the salt itself. Mix and Output are quarter-size siblings; Output also anchors at 12.
MODE & CURVE
Two cell pairs, not two dropdowns. Curve cells carry their own salt sample (three chunky flakes vs a fine drift); Mode cells carry mirrored arrows — Acid lifts, Fat sinks — and the active mode's sentence sits right under them in ink. Tooltips on all five controls.
TYPE
Caveat Brush for the wordmark and every numeral, Patrick Hand SC for painted caps legends, Patrick Hand for prose — same pair as Capsaicin and Schmear.
JUCE NOTES
620 × 480 base, uniform scale. Flat fills and 2–3.4px strokes only, no gradients or bevels. Dish = 2 wobbly ellipses + one arc Path + a seeded crystal loop (seed fixed, so grains never dance). Cells = 4 wobbly rounded Paths; only the active cell's fill changes. Values stay live text.
