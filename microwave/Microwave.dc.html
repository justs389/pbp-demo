PALETTE
Graphite appliance plastic 5a5f5c off the real unit, chrome strip d5d0c2, silk-screen chalk ece7db, ink 1b1e1d. The box art comes onto the panel: a crimson b93f52 block behind the wordmark with the cream and teal 2f8d86 swoosh rules struck across it, teal 6fc6bc silk-screen legends, crimson badge line on the chrome. Crimson is heat and engagement, teal is print and WILD. Flat fills, 2.4px wobbled strokes, no bevels.
THE WALL
DRIVE and WOBBLE draw noon as a real landmark: a heavy tick outside the ring, ink arc up to it, crimson arc past it, and the readout turns crimson with "past the wall". OUTPUT draws from noon outward in either direction, never a min-to-max sweep.
CHAIN
A path is printed on the plastic above the keys: the raised mic boss → mic tap → TAPE → speaker tap → out arrow. The MIC key is wired to the first stub, SPEAKER to the last; the printed path stays inert; the keys and the note below it carry the state. The handwritten note beside it names the current path.
WILD
Knob and key share the fourth column and are joined by a dashed bracket. Key off: knob greyed, bracket dashed grey, note reads "off — key it on". Key on: bracket teal and solid, knob live, key face teal — the panel stays still.
MOTION
The transport has inertia — the reels ease into speed changes instead of stepping. Both turn the same way, the fuller reel turns slower, both packs are wound the same, a hair apart so they never lock. WOBBLE modulates their speed and drifts the whole shell in a slow wandering orbit — three incommensurate rates, so it never repeats on a beat — with a hair of rotation on top. Past noon on DRIVE the RECORD/BATT lamp swells and throws a pulsing halo across the crimson block — the wall, felt rather than metered. Bypass stops the transport and washes the panel out. No VU: the unit never had one.
JUCE NOTES
900 × 700 base, uniform 0.5–2×, nothing finer than 2px. Every shape is a Path: wobbled rects/circles from a seeded jitter + Catmull-Rom, drawn once into a cached plate image; only knobs, reels, keys, chain and speckles redraw. Grille is a dot loop, not a texture. Caveat Brush for wordmark and values, Patrick Hand SC for legends, Patrick Hand for prose — same pair as Capsaicin and Schmear. Tooltips on every control, toggled by the "?" on the chrome strip.
