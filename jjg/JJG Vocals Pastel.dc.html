THE LUNCHBOX
A kraft-tan rack holding six 500-series faceplates in signal order. The first slot is the 250-series pair — PRE over TAPE — with the DRIVE knob on a paper bridge that straddles the seam. Every faceplate is drawn in the colour of the circuit chosen on its name plate, so the rack recolours itself as you pick models.
KNOB SHAPES FOLLOW THE PLATE
PRE: 511 black US skirt · 73 red Marconi on knurled grey · TG red chicken-head · 60s fluted bakelite. TAPE: 800 brushed silver · 37 slate · Cassette white plastic · 102 bakelite. COMP: 76 wears Capsaicin HOT lavender with the big skirted knob · 2A / 6A grey with bakelite; the two chained modes split the plate top/bottom in signal order. EQ: 70s voicing = coloured SSL-style caps (red HF, brown LF), 80s = Neve-style grey knurl (blue LF); High Mid is always an API knob; Low Mid is the dual-concentric — one arc for the cut, a nub on the ring for frequency. DS: 536A steel-blue with a black knob, 902 charcoal with the yellow dbx knob. SALT is the pinch dish from Salt, Kosher navy or Pink peach.
POWER & METERS
Ten stage-power switches drawn as little slide toggles beside each knob; an off stage drops to 35% ink. Gain reduction fills downward from 0 dB in the module's accent, turning red past −10. The gate lamp glows while it attenuates. Nothing else meters.
JUCE NOTES
1200 × 710 base (landscape suits six modules), uniform scale 0.6–1.5. Flat fills and 2–4 px strokes, no gradients or bevels; every knob is one closed wobbly Path plus a pointer, so each shape is a small draw routine keyed off the plate's choice. Caveat Brush for wordmark, plate names and all numerals; Patrick Hand SC for legends; Patrick Hand for tooltips. Values stay live text.
